# Vox Style
Editorial explainer collage: cut-paper, photo cutouts, flat vectors, and graphic icons on kraft or bold color-blocked fields. Saturated accents on neutrals; crisp silhouettes; tactile paper grain, halftone, and torn edges; information-first hierarchy.

Vary the storytelling device per beat—pick what best clarifies the action: paper diorama, isometric map, labeled diagram, before/after split, magnified detail inset, kinetic cutout type, archival photo collage, or simple character tableau. One clear idea per frame; readable visual hierarchy; light handmade texture without clutter.

No photoreal live-action, slick 3D CGI, generic stock-illustration gloss, or busy multi-idea boards.

## Writing guidance
Explainer voice. One idea per beat: state the claim, then the clarifying detail. Clear, informative, and concrete. Prefer labels and facts over mood; no purple prose or cinematic scene-painting.
