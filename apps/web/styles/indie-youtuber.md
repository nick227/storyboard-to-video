# Indie Youtuber
Photoreal indie creator talking-head: podcast desk or casual room, soft natural window light, bright friendly contrast, handheld vlog energy, expressive face and mic-forward presence.

Vary framing per beat to match the talk—fullscreen face cam for reactions and punchlines; punchy YouTube-thumbnail close-up for hooks and big claims; mid-shot desk-and-mic when setting context; picture-in-picture host cam over subject B-roll, screens, props, or maps when explaining. Keep the same recognizable host; cut organically between intimate face time and subject coverage.

No illustration, painterly rendering, cinematic film-look, or anonymous stock-model polish.

## Writing guidance
Conversational creator voice. Hook fast, react honestly, then explain. Sound like a host talking to camera: punchy claims, personal asides, clear takeaways. Avoid literary narration and film-score atmosphere.
