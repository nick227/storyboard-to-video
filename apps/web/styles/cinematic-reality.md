# Cinematic Reality
Epic sweeping cinematography, photoreal live-action, grand scale and glorious scope, natural motivated light, rich filmic color grading, visible grain and lens character.

Compose in depth: a distinct foreground element, a clear midground hero subject, and an atmospheric background — haze, bokeh, or falloff — separating the layers. Light the hero subject as the unmistakable focal point with a directional key light and rim or backlight for separation; let contrast and shadow fall away from it toward the edges. One clear subject commands the frame; everything else recedes in scale, sharpness, or light.

No illustration, cartoon, or flat 2D rendering; no plastic CGI sheen or airbrushed skin; no flat, shadowless, or evenly lit compositions.
