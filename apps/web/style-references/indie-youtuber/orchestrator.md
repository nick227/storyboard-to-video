# Indie YouTuber — composition
Intent: vlog energy. Each scene is one talk beat or one subject beat.

Unit: one face-cam energy or one B-roll/demo subject.
Cut on: hook, reaction, new explanation, or B-roll/demo switch.
Prefer: one topic energy per card; give reactions their own beat when they reset the tone.
Never: glue a reaction and a new topic into the same segment.
Watch: too few → rambling vlog walls; too many → twitchy cuts that feel unedited.
