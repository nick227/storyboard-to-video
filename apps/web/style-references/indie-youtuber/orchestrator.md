# Indie YouTuber — composition
Intent: vlog energy. Each scene is one talk beat or one subject beat.

Unit: one face-cam energy, one B-roll/demo subject, or one reaction hold.
Cut on: hook, reaction, new explanation, B-roll/demo switch, or a pause that resets energy.
Prefer: one topic energy per card; give reactions and bridge beats their own card when they reset the tone.
Never: glue a reaction and a new topic into the same segment; never invent silent vlog cards.
Watch: too few → rambling vlog walls; too many → twitchy cuts that feel unedited.
