# Dark Gothic — composition
Intent: scare focus. Each scene isolates one wrong detail or threat move.

Unit: one scare focus (threat detail, sound cue, reveal, reaction, or wrong image).
Cut on: the moment the dread target changes — even in the same room — including uneasy holds before a line.
Prefer: short uneasy cards; split when the camera of fear would reframe; allow reaction/bridge beats when the narration carries dread between dialogue.
Never: keep a long dread passage as one card after the focus has moved; never invent empty scare cards.
Watch: too few → scare soup; too many → jump-cut fatigue that dilutes dread.
