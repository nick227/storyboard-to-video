# History Education — composition
Intent: Ken Burns study. Each scene holds one archival image long enough to read.

Unit: one artifact, portrait, map detail, document excerpt, place establish, or brief bridge to the next claim.
Cut on: new figure, new place, new date/claim, zoom into a detail the narration names, or a short hold when the argument needs breath.
Prefer: establish the source → examine one detail → advance the claim; linger rather than micro-cut.
Never: merge two historical claims into one segment; never invent empty silent cards; never rush past a named document or face.
Watch: too few → crowded multi-idea frames; too many → single-word flashes that break the documentary pace.
