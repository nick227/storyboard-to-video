# Vox Style — composition
Intent: explainer clarity. Each scene teaches one idea or diagram step.

Unit: one teaching point, labeled step, before/after, clarifying inset, or brief bridge to the next claim.
Cut on: new claim, new labeled step, clarifying visual, or a short hold before the next point when narration supports it.
Prefer: one idea per segment; clean step cards the viewer can pause on.
Never: merge two teaching points into one segment; never invent empty explainer cards.
Watch: too few → crowded explainers; too many → single-word cards that stall the argument.
