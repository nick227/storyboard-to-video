# Vox Style — composition
Intent: explainer clarity. Each scene teaches one idea or diagram step.

Unit: one teaching point, labeled step, before/after, or clarifying inset.
Cut on: new claim, new labeled step, or a clarifying visual that needs its own card.
Prefer: one idea per segment; clean step cards the viewer can pause on.
Never: merge two teaching points into one segment.
Watch: too few → crowded explainers; too many → single-word cards that stall the argument.
